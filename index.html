01 / PRINCIPLE

Email should be infrastructure,
not another landlord.

Conventional hosting charges per mailbox while keeping operations opaque. Sovereign Mail is being built around domains you own, resources you control, and migration paths you can reverse.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

One cockpit for the entire mail estate.

Separate the public surface, operator controls, and delivery infrastructure without losing one coherent system.

DOMAIN OPERATIONS01

Every domain, visible at once.

Mailboxes, aliases, catch-all policy, DNS readiness, reputation signals, storage, and delivery health in one operator view.

SAFE MIGRATION02

No blind MX cutovers.

Capture the current state, generate a migration plan, verify prerequisites, and retain a tested rollback record.

DELIVERABILITY03

Configuration is not proof.

Test real delivery against major providers, inspect authentication, and block production claims until messages arrive.
