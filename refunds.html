REFUNDS

A short, clear path if AI is not a fit.

An AI plan can be cancelled without disabling Sovereign Mail or changing access to customer-owned data.

Effective August 18, 2026
Prices shown in CAD

Monthly subscriptions

Cancel at any time through the billing portal. Cancellation prevents the next renewal and AI access normally continues until the end of the current paid period.

First-charge refund

You may request a refund of the first monthly AI-plan charge within 14 calendar days when no more than ten percent of that period's included credits have been used. Include the workspace installation identifier and Stripe receipt identifier, but never include credentials, prompts, model responses, or mail content.

Later charges

Renewal periods already started are normally non-refundable. Duplicate charges, billing errors, fraudulent charges, and refunds required by law will be corrected. If the paid AI features are materially unavailable, a fair credit or refund may be offered after review.

How to request one

Use the private billing path described on Support within the applicable period. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Stripe and the card network determine when the credit appears.

Payment failure or a refund changes only paid AI access. It never locks mail, accounts, source code, backup, recovery, or self-hosting.
